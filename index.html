NODEMONKES

The Nodemonkes are 10,000 unique collectible monkes — each one a 28×28-pixel character written directly onto the Bitcoin blockchain in February 2023 as ordinals inscriptions #83,522–#111,319, making them among the first 10k profile-picture collections to live entirely on-chain. There are no servers and no separate image files: every monke is part of Bitcoin itself, as permanent as the chain that carries it. They are the work of two pseudonymous artists, Nodetoshi and Monketoshi, who drew on Bitcoin's technology and economics, the ordinals protocol, libertarian values, and the wider culture of digital art and NFTs.

Within the strict 28×28 grid the variation is endless — no two monkes share the same combination of body, traits and colors. Monkedex is a non-custodial marketplace for them: every trade is a wallet-signed PSBT settled on-chain, custody outputs always keep a maker-side recovery path, and this site never holds your keys or your monkes. HOW TO TRADE

  1. BROWSE. Every monke, trait and rank is one click deep — try Clean, rank:1 or attributes.
  2. CONNECT. Unisat, Xverse, Leather or OKX — the site only passes PSBTs to your wallet; keys never leave it.
  3. LIST / BUY / BID. Listings are seller-signed PSBTs; buys settle as validated transaction packages. Bids are fully-signed purchase transactions the seller can accept at any time — no escrow, ever.
  4. SETTLE ON-CHAIN. Bitcoin is the source of truth. The moment a trade confirms, ownership here follows the chain.

FAQ

Is this custodial? No. The server indexes and coordinates; it can never move your coins or monkes.

What are the fees? Maker 0% / taker 0% — you pay only Bitcoin network fees.

What about front-running? Offers and bids commit both parties to the exact transaction up front — per-flow guarantees on the trust-model page.
